I am the founding editor-in-chief of [In Development](https://www.indevelopmentmag.com/). I am also a non-resident fellow at the [Energy for Growth Hub](https://energyforgrowth.org/), the [Centre for British Progress](https://britishprogress.org/), and the [Roots of Progress Institute](https://rootsofprogress.org/).

I write about migration, growth, and cost-effective policy wins at [Lauren Policy](https://www.laurenpolicy.com/). Outside of my Substack, my work has been published in **[The Economist](https://www.economist.com/graphic-detail/2024/12/06/taylor-swift-imperfect-capitalist)**, **Works in Progress** ([1](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/every-generator-is-a-policy-failure/), [2](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/the-end-of-lead/)), **Asterisk Magazine** ([1](https://asteriskmag.com/issues/04/is-our-children-learning), [2](https://asteriskmag.com/issues/09/a-defense-of-weird-research), [3](https://asteriskmag.substack.com/p/forecast-the-impact-of-usaid-nutrition)), **[Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2025/02/05/pepfar-trump-lifesaving-hiv-aids-soft-power-danger/)**, and **The Washington Post** ([1](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2020/07/04/if-fire-saga-really-wanted-win-eurovision-this-is-what-they-needed-know/), [2](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2022/05/16/ukraines-eurovision-victory-was-political-so-is-eurovision-itself/)).

Previously, I ran the Horizon Scanning Study Group at Renaissance Philanthropy, where I coordinated a network of scientists and technologists assessing UK science priorities. Before that, I was a research fellow at Open Philanthropy.

My academic background is in political science and physics. I was a PhD student at UC San Diego from 2018–2021, where my research focused on violence and its societal effects. Before that, I was an astrophysicist — I hold an MS in physics from UCSD and a BS from Caltech, where I worked with George Fuller on theoretical neutrino physics and cosmology.

I have also done various other things with my life. From 2016–2024, I was a QI elf, writing for [@qikipedia](https://twitter.com/qikipedia) and contributing to books like 222 QI Answers to Your Quite Ingenious Questions. I've appeared on BBC Radio 2 sixteen times. I hold [two](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2022139636A1/en?inventor=lauren+gilbert&oq=lauren+) [patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2022233405A1/en?inventor=Lauren+Ann+GILBERT) in extended reality technology. And I am a Stanford Linear Accelerator Center Qualified Laser Operator, which has come in handy exactly zero times since leaving Caltech.

When not working or writing, I am generally being bullied by my cat, [Lyra](https://s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/lgilbert.co/lyra.jpg).
